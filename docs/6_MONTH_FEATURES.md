# GoallineIQ — 6-Month Feature Roadmap

## Month 1: Tournament Countdown

- **Days to WC2026 kickoff** — Hero countdown banner on the home page.
- **Group draw viewer** — All 12 groups with current Elo ratings and model group-winner probabilities.
- **Qualifying tracker** — Which teams have/haven't qualified; updated as qualifiers are confirmed.
- **Host nation spotlight** — USA, Canada, Mexico — home advantage analysis.

## Month 2: Team Intelligence

- **Team profile pages** — Elo history, squad depth, key players, recent form, World Cup history.
- **Style of play indicator** — Possession-based vs. counter-attack flag based on historical stats.
- **Head-to-head history** — All-time World Cup meetings between any two nations.

## Month 3: Odds & Betting

- **Match odds comparison** — Model probability vs. nightly snapshot odds vs. best market.
- **Group stage value finder** — Edge > 3% on group stage match winners.
- **Golden Boot odds** — Top scorer probability chart vs. DraftKings market.
- **Tournament winner futures** — Model champion probability vs. outright market.

## Month 4: Bracket Simulation

- **Monte Carlo bracket** — 10,000-simulation advancement probabilities for all 48 teams.
- **Knockout path visualiser** — Each team's most likely path through the bracket to the final.
- **Upset probability map** — Heat-map of most likely R16 upsets.

## Month 5: Match Day (During Tournament)

- **Live scoreboard** — Auto-refresh during matches via ESPN API.
- **In-play odds tracker** — Capture odds snapshots every 15 minutes during live group stage.
- **Real-time bracket update** — After each result, re-run simulator and update advancement probabilities.

## Month 6: Analytics & Reporting

- **Group stage accuracy report** — After group stage completes, publish model accuracy.
- **Tournament recap** — Post-WC report: model picks vs. actual results, biggest upsets predicted.
- **Daily email during tournament** — Day-of match picks with odds and model probabilities.
